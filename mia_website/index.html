Who we are

About Maternal Initiative Africa

MIA works to help close gaps in maternal, newborn and child health through community engagement, professional collaboration, health education and practical partnerships.

Our mission

To connect health professionals, communities and partners to improve access to maternal, newborn and child health education, support, referrals and services in underserved African communities.

Our vision

Communities across Africa where every mother and child can access the information, support and quality care they need to thrive.

Our approach

We listen to communities, work alongside nurses and midwives, use evidence to guide action, and collaborate with local institutions to develop sustainable solutions.

Our core values

Our work is guided by principles that place mothers, children and communities at the centre.

What we do

Programs & Projects

Our program areas respond to practical barriers that mothers, children and frontline health workers may face in underserved communities.

♡

Maternal & Newborn Health

Promoting pregnancy health education, birth preparedness, postnatal support and timely care-seeking.

⌁

Digital Health & Telehealth

Exploring responsible digital education, follow-up, guidance and referral support to connect families with care.

＋

Community Outreach

Working with community partners to identify local needs, share information and strengthen referral pathways.

♧

Nurses & Midwives Capacity Building

Supporting peer learning, professional connection and opportunities to strengthen skills and practice.

☼

Maternal Mental Health

Increasing awareness of emotional wellbeing during pregnancy and after birth, and encouraging appropriate support.

⌕

Research, Data & Advocacy

Promoting evidence-informed decisions and amplifying community perspectives to improve maternal and child health.
