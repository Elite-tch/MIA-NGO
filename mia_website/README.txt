MATERNAL INITIATIVE AFRICA (MIA) — SIMPLE WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: Complete responsive single-page website.

How to preview:
1. Open index.html in a modern web browser.
2. To publish, upload index.html to your hosting provider's public website folder (often public_html) or use a static hosting service.

Before publishing:
- Replace the placeholder email address with a verified, active MIA email.
- Add approved Board of Directors and leadership profiles.
- Replace impact prompts with verified figures only.
- Add official social media URLs, office details, reports, policies and approved photos.
- Connect forms to a secure backend or approved form service if you want inquiries handled without relying on the visitor's email app.
- Confirm that all images used from Unsplash are suitable for your intended use and can load online.

This is a front-end starter website. It does not include a database, administrator dashboard, user accounts, or a hosted domain.
